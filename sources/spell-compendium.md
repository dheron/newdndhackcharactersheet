## **Cantrips**

### **Control Flames** \| Cantrip • Transmutation \[instant or 1 hr\] \[Wi, Dr, Ba\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** 60 ft **Comp:** S Affect a nonmagical flame that fits in a 5 ft cube: make it spread 5 ft, put it out, change its light or color, or make simple shapes appear in it. Up to 3 non-instant effects can be active at once.

### **Dancing Lights** \| Cantrip • Evocation \[1 min\] \[Wi, Ba\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** 120 ft **Comp:** V, S, M (phosphorus or firefly) Create four torch-sized lights, each shedding dim light in a 10 ft radius. You can move them up to 60 ft as an action, or combine them into one glowing humanoid form.

### **Druidcraft** \| Cantrip • Transmutation \[Dr\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** 30 ft **Comp:** V, S Create a harmless sensory effect, predict the weather for the next 24 hours, light or snuff out a candle or torch, or make a flower bloom.

### **Friends** \| Cantrip • Enchantment \[1 min\] \[Wi, Ba\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** Self **Comp:** S, M (makeup or charm) You have advantage on Cha checks against one creature. It knows you used magic on it when the spell ends.

### **Guidance** \| Cantrip • Divination \[1 min\] \[Cl\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** Touch **Comp:** V, S The target subtracts **1d4** from the roll of one ability check it makes before the spell ends.

### **Gust** \| Cantrip • Transmutation \[Wi, Dr\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** 30 ft **Comp:** V, S Choose one: push a Medium or smaller creature 5 ft away from you **(Blast/Barrage save)**, or no effect; push an unattended object of up to 5 lb 10 ft away from you; or create a harmless sensory effect with wind.

### **Light** \| Cantrip • Evocation \[1 hr\] \[Wi, Cl, Ba\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** Touch **Comp:** V, M (firefly or phosphorescent moss) An object sheds bright light in a 20 ft radius and dim light for another 20 ft. Covering it blocks the light. Casting the spell again ends the first casting.

### **Mage Hand** \| Cantrip • Conjuration \[1 min\] \[Wi, Ba\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** 30 ft **Comp:** V, S A spectral hand appears within range. It can handle or carry up to 10 lb. It can't attack or activate magic items.

### **Mending** \| Cantrip • Transmutation \[Wi, Cl, Dr, Ba\]

**Cast:** 1 minute • **Range:** Touch **Comp:** V, S, M (2 lodestones) Repair one break or tear no larger than 1 ft in an object. It can't restore magic to a magic item.

### **Message** \| Cantrip • Transmutation \[1 round\] \[Wi, Ba\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** 120 ft **Comp:** V, S, M (short copper wire) Whisper a message that only the target hears. It can whisper a reply. The message passes through solid objects, but not through 1 ft of stone, 1 inch of metal, a thin sheet of lead or 3 ft of wood.

### **Minor Illusion** \| Cantrip • Illusion \[1 min\] \[Wi, Ba\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** 30 ft **Comp:** S, M (bit of fleece) Create an image of an object that fits in a 5 ft cube, or a sound. A creature can use an action to make an Int check. On a success, it sees through the illusion.

### **Mold Earth** \| Cantrip • Transmutation \[instant or 1 hr\] \[Wi, Dr\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** 30 ft **Comp:** S Affect loose earth or stone in a 5 ft cube: dig it out and move it up to 5 ft, make shapes or colors appear on it, or turn it into difficult terrain or back. Up to 2 non-instant effects can be active at once.

### **Prestidigitation** \| Cantrip • Transmutation \[up to 1 hr\] \[Wi, Ba\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** 10 ft **Comp:** V, S Create a minor magical effect: light or snuff out a candle or torch, clean or soil an object, chill, warm or flavor food, make a small mark or symbol, or make a harmless sensory effect.

### **Resistance** \| Cantrip • Abjuration \[1 min\] \[Cl\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** Touch **Comp:** V, S, M (miniature cloak) The target adds **1d4** to one saving throw it makes before the spell ends.

### **Shape Water** \| Cantrip • Transmutation \[instant or 1 hr\] \[Dr\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** 30 ft **Comp:** S Affect nonmagical water in a 5 ft cube: move it up to 5 ft, shape it into simple forms, change its color or clarity, or freeze it if no creatures are in it. Up to 2 non-instant effects can be active at once.

### **Spare the Dying** \| Cantrip • Necromancy \[Cl\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** Touch **Comp:** V, S A living creature at 0 hp becomes stable. No effect on undead or constructs.

### **Thaumaturgy** \| Cantrip • Transmutation \[up to 1 min\] \[Cl\]

**Cast:** 1 action • **Range:** 30 ft **Comp:** V Create a minor wonder: your voice booms three times louder, the ground trembles harmlessly, an unlocked door or window flies open or slams shut, a flame flickers or changes color, or a harmless sound rings out.
