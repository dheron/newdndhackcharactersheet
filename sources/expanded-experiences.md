# Expanded Experiences

Options for players to pick from, or to use as models for writing their own. None of these lists is complete.

## How specific an Experience is changes how it works

The narrower the Experience, the less often it needs a roll. The broader the Experience, the more often it applies, and the more often the DM still calls for a check.

- **Specific.** When the task sits squarely inside the Experience and the stakes are low, the DM can rule that no roll is needed. A character with Knot Tying does not roll to judge whether a knot will hold a given weight.  
- **Background or Profession.** Covers a wider range of tasks. The DM still calls for a check, and the Experience gives \+2 to the ability score. A character with Sailor makes an Intelligence check to judge whether that knot will hold, with \+2 to Intelligence.  
- **Short Phrase.** Applies when the situation matches what the phrase says about the character. The DM judges how far it reaches, and the player says how it applies before rolling.

## Section 4: Short Phrases

The words in brackets show roughly where the phrase could apply. They are a starting point for the player and DM to agree on, not a limit.

### Grit

- I've Had Worse (shrugging off pain, cold and hunger)  
- Get Up Again (recovering from knocks and setbacks)  
- Dig In (holding a position, standing firm)  
- Last One Standing (outlasting others)  
- Nothing to Lose (desperate gambits, reckless courage)  
- Born in the Gutter (street hardship and getting by)  
- Hard Winters Taught Me (cold, scarcity, rationing)  
- Pain Is Information (reading injuries, your own and others')  
- Never Turn Your Back (alertness to ambush and betrayal)

### Cunning

- Always Have an Exit (finding ways out)  
- Trust the Plan (preparation and contingencies)  
- There's Always a Back Door (finding another way in)  
- Measure Twice (care, precision, checking work)  
- Everyone Has a Price (bribery, finding leverage)  
- Read the Room (sensing mood and danger in a crowd)  
- Smile and Nod (going unnoticed socially)  
- Never Show Your Hand (bluffing, keeping secrets)  
- A Little Bird Told Me (rumours and informants)  
- I Know a Guy (contacts and favours)  
- You Owe Me One (calling in debts)  
- Trust, But Verify (checking claims and stories)  
- Follow the Money (financial trails and motives)  
- Cheat to Win (tricks, rigging, sharp practice)  
- Talk My Way Out (bluffing or defusing trouble)  
- One Last Job (planning and pulling off heists)  
- Expect the Worst (spotting traps and betrayals)

### Loyalty and heart

- Leave No One Behind (rescues, carrying the wounded)  
- Family Comes First (protecting kin and dependents)  
- My Word Is My Bond (oaths, being believed)  
- Honour the Dead (graves, rites, respect for the fallen)  
- Lead from the Front (inspiring others by example)  
- Keep Them Calm (steadying the frightened)  
- Kind Words Cost Nothing (comfort, goodwill)  
- Pay It Forward (earning goodwill from strangers)  
- Open Door (hospitality, offering shelter)  
- Friends in Every Port (finding allies in travel)

### Wits and learning

- Read the Fine Print (contracts, hidden terms, tricky wording)  
- Everything Has a Pattern (puzzles, codes, cycles)  
- Question Everything (spotting holes in a story)  
- I Read About This Once (half-remembered lore)  
- The Old Ways (traditions and folk practice)  
- Seen It Before (recognizing a repeated trick)  
- Learn by Doing (using unfamiliar tools)  
- Make It Work (improvised repairs)  
- Waste Not (salvage and reuse)  
- Curiosity Pays (poking at odd things)  
- Every Lock Has a Key (finding the intended way past)

### Swagger and nerve

- Fortune Favours the Bold (gambles and charges)  
- Look Them in the Eye (staring down threats)  
- Make an Entrance (arriving with impact)  
- Smooth as Silk (graceful talk under pressure)  
- Never Let Them See You Sweat (composure)  
- Wing It (improvising)  
- No Plan Survives (adapting when things go wrong)  
- Louder Is Better (commanding a room)
