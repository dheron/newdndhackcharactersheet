## Gaps and interpretations

Where the docs are silent or the app has to read them a certain way. Each needs David's confirmation.

- Armour: the sheet writes AC as '11 + Dex. Mod.'. The app uses the DEX AC Bonus from Ability Score Tables v4 as the Dex. Mod.
- Armour: Heavy armour lists a flat AC. The app adds no DEX bonus to Heavy armour.
- Starting equipment names are matched to the tables: 'Light Cross' = Light Crossbow, 'Longsword' = Long Sword, 'Shortsword' = Short Sword, 'Shortbow' = Short Bow, 'Learther Armour' = Leather.
- A name with a slash is two items sharing a row (David): Chain Mail and Banded Mail, Scale Mail and Brigandine, Ranseur and Pike are separate items with the row's values.
- Starting equipment: 'Thrown weapon' means a weapon with the Thrown trait; 'One-Handed' means a weapon without the Two-Handed trait; 'Melee' means any weapon that is not a bow, crossbow, sling or dart. Ranger's 'One-Handed Martial' is melee only (David). 'Druid weapon' and 'Wizard Weapons' are the class weapon lists in Classes v4. The full mapping is in src/engine/equipment.ts.
- Attacks: bows, crossbows, slings and darts use DEX to hit and add no damage bonus; every other weapon, including thrown ones, uses STR to hit and adds STR damage (as in the original app).
- AC with no armour: 10 + DEX AC bonus (Barbarian: 10 + double). The 10 comes from the original app, not a doc.
- Fighter Weapon Specialization (+2 damage, melee or ranged) is chosen during creation and added to that weapon type's damage.
- Saves on the sheet: BNS is every bonus that always applies to that save (racial bonuses, and DEX on Blast / Barrage). Total = Base - BNS, the number the d20 must reach, because the Saving Throws doc adds bonuses to the roll. Situational bonuses (WIS vs mind spells, racial vs poison and so on) stay on the note lines.
- Weapon Mag (magic bonus) is added to Hit Bns only, as the formula in the development plan; it is not added to damage.
- Ability scores (from the original app): six rolls by pick order, racial modifiers applied after, then each score held to 6 to 18. Ability Score Tables v4 goes to 24 for racial and magical bonuses.
- Level 1 HP (from the original app): maximum of the class hit die plus the CON HP bonus; Barbarian doubles the CON bonus.
- Text is copied as written, including doc typos (for example 'Learther', 'You are immunity to disease', 'Thieves Cant’').

- **Fighter:** A multi-classed fighter can use all of his abilities without restriction.
- **Ranger:** A multi-classed Ranger may only benefit from Two-Handed Fighting, Natural Explorer, and Spellcasting if they are not wearing Heavy Armour.
- **Barbarian:** A multi-classed Barbarian may only benefit from class abilities if they are unarmoured.
- **Thief:** A multi-classed Thief may only benefit from Backstab or Evasion if they are not wearing Medium or Heavy Armour
- **Bard:** A multi-classed Bard may only cast spells if they are not wearing Heavy Armour
- **Cleric:** A multi-classed Cleric can use all of his abilities without restriction.
- **Druid:** A multi-classed may only wear non metal amour
- **Wizard:** A multi-classed Wizard may only cast spells if they are unarmoured.
